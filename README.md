# Craft Journey

[![License: GPL v3](https://img.shields.io/badge/License-GPLv3-blue.svg)](LICENSE)

*[Version française](README.fr.md)*

A **World of Warcraft: Forever** addon that tracks your crafting orders across all your characters.

> 🚧 **Work in progress** — not released yet.

## What it does

Pick a recipe, set how many copies you want, and Craft Journey shows the reagents you need on screen, like a quest tracker: `owned / needed`, counting bags and bank.

- **Full reagent chain**: crafting 4 bags? Craft Journey works out the bolts of cloth *and* the raw cloth behind them. The chain stops at the first reagent no character of yours can craft.
- **Shared across your characters**: any character can follow any order. Create an order on your tailor, follow it on your warrior, and see how much cloth is left to farm.
- **Per-character totals**: each character's tracker adds up every order it follows.
